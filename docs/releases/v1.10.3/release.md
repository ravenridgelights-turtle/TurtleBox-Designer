## What's new in v{{VERSION}}

<!-- turtlebox:changes:start -->
- **Bud mounting corrected:** all 115 NBF profiles were checked against the original STEP models. Raised inside corners and other usable posts are selectable. Plate holes, screw recesses, spacer heights and the hardware list follow your choices. The previous floor-post pattern remains available; reference markers explain outside recesses and shallow bores.
- **See the mounts in 3D:** click posts in the box, adjust real plate elevation and use **See spacer** to zoom onto a mount. The spacer-height edit now stays applied.
- **USB accessories snap in:** purple plug/host markers, correct insertion, automatic orientation and attachment that follows the board. Grab the antenna to drag the adapter; click it to fold. Grab either PSU to move a stack.
- **Wiring has its own 3D workspace:** gauge, colors, slack, Ethernet and pigtails are directly below the view bar. Single-click draws once; double-click repeats. Reach lower supplies behind other parts, select a cable and press Delete, or insert a pigtail by clicking its wall opening.
- **Height checks match the model:** Bud clearance includes documented closed-lid space, and adapter height follows the rendered antenna and USB socket. Critical problems are red; measurement warnings are yellow.
- **Try powering your layout:** add the 16 AWG AC cord through PG9, snap the plug into the matching country outlet and switch ON. Correct AC and DC connections start fans, indicators, brain activity and the original Kulp boot screen. Crossed connections produce simulated blue smoke. This is a connectivity preview.
- **Print the stack supports with the plate:** uprights are joined vertically to their plate pieces. The splitter keeps whole uprights away from seams and checks printer height. Each printable piece has its own watertight STL in one ZIP. Optional separate frames and laser files remain available.
- **Parts list moved up:** find hardware, cord, wire sizes, colors, slack, pigtails, Ethernet cables, glands, material estimates and purchase links beside **3D assembly**.
